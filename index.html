How the model works

1 · Goal expectancy (xG)

Each team's attack and defence strength is combined with the league baseline to produce expected goals. Defensive quality reduces the opponent's xG.

2 · Poisson scorelines

Expected goals feed a Poisson distribution to compute the probability of every scoreline from 0-0 up to 8-8, then aggregated into 1X2, BTTS and totals markets.

3 · Elo tilt

World-Football Elo ratings nudge the goal means so a large rating gap is reflected even when raw scoring profiles look similar.

4 · Monte-Carlo

20,000 simulated matches stress-test the analytic result; the two are averaged for a more robust final probability.

5 · Fair odds & value

Probabilities invert to fair decimal odds, compared against a synthesised bookmaker line to flag where an edge would exist.

6 · Form & history

Recent W/D/L momentum and head-to-head records contextualise the numbers with the human side of the matchup.
