🧮 How the model works

1 · Goal expectancy

Real attack/defence strengths (calibrated to 2026 results) combine with the tournament scoring baseline to produce each side's expected goals.

2 · Poisson scorelines

Expected goals feed a Poisson distribution for every scoreline 0-0 → 8-8, aggregated into 1X2, BTTS and totals.

3 · Elo tilt

World-Football Elo (Spain 2129, Uruguay 1890, Cape Verde 1625, Saudi 1593) nudges the goal means toward the stronger side.

4 · Monte-Carlo

20,000 simulated matches stress-test the maths; analytic and simulated probabilities are averaged.

5 · Match simulator

Each "Kick off" samples a fresh scoreline from the model and plays it out minute-by-minute with live events, stats and commentary.

6 · Real context

FIFA rankings, Elo, current form, the actual Group H results and head-to-head history all feed the picture.
